#### Spatiotemporal modeling:

Initially, the idea was to use a 3D spline representation.
For orbits with ~300 frames and high resolution this results in ~350,000 model parameters.
Alternatives were explored to introduce empirical basis functions to replace the 2D spatial spline:
- PCA - Cannot handle NaN
- Iterative PCA - Implicit imputation
- VAE - Descrete basis functions

Neural network function approximation using sinus activation functions is a potential option as smooth and infinitely differentiable solutions are achieved.
The downside is non-trivial posterior covariance and indirect control of spatial resolution.
Spectral implicit neural representation fixes the spatial resolution, but is slower and current implementation relies on ReLU or tanh and not sinus.
